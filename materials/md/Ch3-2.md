§4.2 Logic Design Conventions

- Information encoded in binary
  - Low voltage = 0, High voltage = 1
  - One wire per bit
  - Multi-bit data encoded on multi-wire buses
- Combinational element
  - Operate on data
  - Output is a function of input
- State (sequential) elements
  - Store information

## Slide 26 — Exercise 3 — Clock Period & Delay

Exercise 3 — Clock Period & Delay

Consider the clocked data path below. Ignore register-related delays.

Register A

ALU
4 ns

Mux
2 ns

Register B

(a) Total delay?     (b) Minimum clock period?     (c) What if Tclk = 5 ns?

Answer

(a) Total combinational delay = 4 ns + 2 ns = 6 ns

(b) Minimum clock period = 6 ns

(c) 5 ns < 6 ns → the result may not reach Register B before the next clock edge.

The longest combinational delay determines the minimum clock period.
